## Medium (Blog/Forum - 3 paragraphs)

**KerbNoteLite v1.3.0 - Major Performance Update**

The latest version of KerbNoteLite brings two powerful new alarm systems and massive performance improvements. The new Terrain Proximity Alarm System (GPWS) provides aviation-style warnings including Pull-Up alerts, terrain ahead prediction, gear warnings with roll suppression, altitude callouts (200m down to 10m), and sink rate warnings. Never crash into terrain again!

The Resource Monitoring system keeps track of all your vessel's critical resources with customizable thresholds. Get warned before running out of ElectricCharge, LiquidFuel, Oxidizer, MonoPropellant, and more. It even monitors your EVA jetpack fuel and communication signal strength. Each resource can be configured independently with thresholds from 0-100%.

Performance improvements are huge: 80-85% reduction in frame time thanks to cached operations, eliminated LINQ allocations, and optimized algorithms. The Global Alarm Panel provides centralized control for all systems with one-click toggles. Your existing notes and alarms are preserved when upgrading. Download now: https://github.com/garyblu71mods/KerbNoteLite
